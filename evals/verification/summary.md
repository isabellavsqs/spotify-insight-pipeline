# Independent verification and disagreement report (full run)

Generated by `python -m pipeline.evals_report` from `grading/analysis/verification.jsonl` and the saved labels. Aggregates: [`summary.json`](summary.json).

## Procedure

- **Verifier:** Claude Haiku 5.5 (`verify-v2`, effort low, thinking off, Message Batches, ≤ 50 reviews per request), a different model family from the Jev labeler.
- **Independence:** the verifier receives the original review text and the shared rubric only, never the first label. Code compares the two answers (`pipeline/verify.py: compare`).
- **Sample:** a 2% stratified random sample, reserved first and declared before the run, then flagged cases (needs_review, severity 5, label combinations the rubric forbids) up to a 5% cap. Agreement is reported separately for each part; only the random part estimates the population.
- **Disagreement** = different topic, different intent, or severity more than 1 apart. Disagreements go to the capped fallback (≤ 1% of texts) in a fixed order; the rest stay flagged `needs_review`.

## What happened to disagreements

- 9,844 of 24,203 verified texts disagreed.
- 4,914 of them carry a fallback label (Claude Haiku 5.5, effort medium): the 4,841 adjudicated within the full run's 1% cap (fact F14) plus 73 reused from fallback answers cached in the development runs. The fallback kept the Jev topic and intent in 1,580 cases, sided with the verifier in 2,260, and gave a third answer in 1,074.
- 4,930 disagreements keep the Jev label and are flagged `needs_review`. The run fact F15 (5,003) also counts the reused answers as not adjudicated in this run.

## Planted wrong labels (synthetic test copy)

Copies of 600 random-sample rows where the submitted label and the verifier agreed. In 300 copies one field is deliberately wrong (topic shifted, intent shifted, or severity moved by 2); 300 are left unchanged. The production comparison code verify.compare() is re-run against the saved verifier answers. The copies are synthetic and excluded from all business results.

| Planted field | Copies | Detected |
|---|---:|---:|

| **Total** | **300** | **295** |

Unchanged copies flagged by mistake: **0 of 300**. Per-case results: [`planted_label_test.json`](planted_label_test.json).

Every planted topic and intent error was caught. The missed severity plants are cases where the planted value is within one level of the verifier's own answer (for example 4 changed to 2 when the verifier said 3): the declared tolerance of ±1 cannot see a two-level error that lands next to the verifier. This is a real limit of the check.
