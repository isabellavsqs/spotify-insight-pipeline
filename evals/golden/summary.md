# Golden-50 evaluation: human labels vs. submitted labels

Generated by `python -m pipeline.evals_report` (offline). Per-case results: [`per_case.csv`](per_case.csv); human labels: [`golden_50_labeled.csv`](golden_50_labeled.csv); aggregates: [`summary.json`](summary.json).

- **Labels compared:** the submitted labels in `grading/records.jsonl.gz` (Jev `enrich-v5`, with the capped Claude fallback where it applied) and the independent verifier (Claude Haiku 5.5, `verify-v2`, which labeled all 50).
- **Use of the human labels:** measurement only. They were written by the student before this evaluation, never entered a prompt, example, routing threshold or issue rule, and nothing was changed after seeing these results.
- **Coverage:** predictions for 50 of 50 reviews; none missing or quarantined.

## Ambiguous and hard cases

- **11 ambiguous cases**, defined as reviews where the human labeler recorded a note explaining a judgment call (2 of them are in an unsupported language). They stay in every metric; none was dropped.
- Topic accuracy is 0.46 on ambiguous cases against 0.80 on clear ones; intent accuracy 0.46 against 1.00.
- **Unsupported language:** the pipeline does not translate. The model labels the text as written; the human labeled these `unclear`. Their model labels are in `per_case.csv`.

## needs_review evaluated as a prediction

`needs_review` is treated as a prediction that the label may be wrong, not as a correctness guarantee. The model flagged 10 of 50 reviews; 17 reviews have a topic or intent error.

- Precision (flagged reviews that really have an error): **0.70**
- Recall (errors that were flagged): **0.41**

## Evidence quotes and entities

- **Exact substring (checked in code):** 50 of 50 evidence quotes are exact substrings of the review text.
- **Agreement with the human's quote:** different 7, overlap 13, same 30. The code picks one sentence deterministically; "different" means another sentence of the same review.
- **Entities:** 3 of 27 extracted entities do not appear literally in the text (3 reviews). They are listed per case in `entities_not_literally_in_text` for inspection.
